EPUB, MOBI and AZW3 are converted here in your browser, pictures included, then sent to the device. PDFs are shown as their pages, scaled to the screen's width: scroll down to read. Plain .txt works too. Books with DRM (copy protection) can't be converted.

Books on the device

Re-process rebuilds a book from its saved original (using the picture style above), handy after converter improvements or to try another style. Books uploaded before pictures were supported only kept their text: upload those again to get their pictures.
